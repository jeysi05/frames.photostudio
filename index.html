Studio Philosophy

Why at FRAMES
we don't have monitors.

When there is a screen in the room, people pose for the screen. They freeze, adjust their posture after every click, and obsess over micro-flaws.

By taking away the live monitor, we restore your presence. You look at the person in front of you, you laugh without second-guessing your smile, and you just have fun.

"The clicker is in your hand. The moment is yours."

Good to know

Common questions

Do I really get unlimited photos?

Yes — everything you shoot is yours. You'll receive a high-resolution Google Drive / private gallery download link within 24 hours.

What if I'm shy about posing?

That's exactly why we don't have monitors or a photographer in the room. Just you, your people, and a clicker.

Can I bring my dog or cat?

Absolutely, and they are completely free. Just check the pet box during booking so we have the room prepped.

Where are you located?

215 Salcedo, Legazpi Village, Makati. Walking distance from Legazpi Active Park and Washington SyCip Park.

What payment methods do you accept?

GCash, Maya, QR Ph, or debit/credit cards through PayMongo. Instant automated confirmation.
